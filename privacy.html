Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

What this prototype is

CareLink Rural is an academic software prototype. It is not a certified medical product and is not intended for real clinical use.

What data is stored

All account details, check-ins, and appointment requests you enter are saved only in your own browser's local storage, on your own device. Nothing is sent to a remote server or database, and no one else — including the project's developers — can see what you enter.

What you should not enter

Please do not enter real names, real medical information, or any other real personal data belonging to yourself or anyone else. Use placeholder or fictional details only.

Clearing your data

You can remove all locally stored data at any time by clearing your browser's site data/local storage for this page, or by using your browser's "Clear browsing data" option.

Contact

Questions about this prototype can be sent to support@carelinkrural.example.
